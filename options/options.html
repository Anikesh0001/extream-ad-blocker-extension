My filters

Your own rules, one per line. Examples: ||ads.example.com^ (block a domain) · example.com##.banner (hide an element) · example.com##+js(set-constant, adblock, false) (neutralize a script) · @@||example.com^ (allow). The element picker adds rules here automatically.

Stuck on a "disable your ad blocker" wall? Add site.com##+js(unwall) — it strips the dark overlay and restores scrolling. You can also force scrolling with site.com##body:style(overflow: auto !important).
